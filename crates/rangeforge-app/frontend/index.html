          DSD Format

          RangeForge cannot write DSD files, and the sample rates and bit depths DSD uses are fundamentally different from those of common formats. These two settings keep you from accidentally transcoding DSD at its source sample rate and bit depth. Each is used only where the Output Format says source.

          Online lookups

          Off by default. Nothing goes online unless this is on.

          RangeForge comes with no Last.fm key. Create your own for an app on Last.fm (free) and paste it here. It is kept in this computer's credential store, not in a file.

          Hot 100 tag

          Songs that charted on the Billboard Hot 100 get this tag (needs Metadata API; no key). The position is the song's best ever: 1 means only number ones, 100 means anything that charted.

          RangeForge

          Measures loudness once, evens out volume with one gain per song, splits CUE albums, tags and writes clean files. Free to use.

          If it saves you time, you can support it on Ko-fi. It is optional and nothing is locked behind it.

          Several tags can be pasted at once, separated by commas, semicolons or line breaks.

          A box left empty keeps what each song has. These names also go on the written files, over what a CUE sheet says (the sheet's split points and track numbers stay).
